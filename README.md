## Welcome to Granger Security

Hi, I'm Bhargav. Granger Security is my YouTube channel, where I explain cyber security ideas in plain English and share what I'm learning as I work towards my first security role.

I'm not a big course creator with a studio. I record short videos about the things I'm studying and building in my own lab, and I keep the mistakes in, because that's usually where the real learning happens.

The channel is for:

* people trying to get into a security operations role
* anyone studying for Security+ who wants a concept explained quickly
* career changers who want to see that the move is possible
* anyone building a home lab on an Apple Silicon Mac, where many tools don't run straight away

I've published more than 230 videos and Shorts since 2022.

## What I'm making right now

At the moment I'm posting a daily series of short explainers in the **[Learn AI Security](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgzSoK4Xo5hnXGebggfe9BUYkywfqWrnW)** playlist. Each one takes a single idea, like SIEM, phishing, ransomware, log analysis, CVEs or zero-days, and explains it in 15 to 30 seconds.

Every Short goes through the same steps:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["Pick one idea"] --> B["Write the opening line<br/>with the definition"]
    B --> C["Record my own<br/>voiceover"]
    C --> D["Edit to 15 to 30 seconds<br/>with captions"]
    D --> E["Clear title and<br/>a few hashtags"]
    E --> F["Publish, then<br/>do it again tomorrow"]
```

I keep it to one idea per video so it's easy to follow, and I post daily because a steady rhythm works better for me than the occasional big video.

## About me

I'm **Bhargav Baranda**. I hold an MSc in Information Security from Royal Holloway, University of London, which is an NCSC-recognised Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Research. I passed CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) in August 2026.

My home lab runs on an Apple Silicon Mac with UTM and Kali Linux, alongside Splunk, Suricata and a Wazuh SIEM on a cloud server. I've written up thirteen labs on GitHub, covering network analysis, threat detection, phishing investigations and vulnerability management. I also built [OZONE Shield](https://ozone-shield.bbaranda055.workers.dev), a free tool that helps people check whether a message is a scam.

I first started the channel in 2022, took a break, and picked it up again in 2026 while studying for Security+ and looking for my first security role. The daily Shorts are how I learn, and I share them as I go.
